TL;DR

Opening night of MILKMAN ZERO (Thu Dec 3) is nearly sold out, and tickets for Fri Dec 4 are running low. There's a party nearby after the opening night show. December 3–13 at Mitu580 in Brooklyn. Code CHUG for $25 tickets.

Hi!

So much going on these days. I got back from Omaha 10 days ago and went straight into rehearsals. It's mostly been me and our director Jerry making changes and really dialing in the show. Over the weekend we had our designer run and it was a great opportunity to show what we've been working on to our incredible creative and producing team. We had a lot of fun ideas and we're going to try and incorporate as many of them as we can.

Things are kicking into gear. Next stop is load-in next weekend where we move into Mitu580, followed by tech.

I had a grand plan to share all these dynamic rehearsal shots and behind the scenes of the team enjoying donuts and throwing around ideas but it turns out it's hard to get photos of all of this when you're the one on stage and you forget to ask someone to take photos during rehearsal. So enjoy these contextless mood board photos from the very early stages of the project. They capture the general vibe of the process over the last week.

I am excited. I am tired. I am nervous. Lots of big fun feelings.

Mostly I'm feeling ready. Hope to see you there.

Matt

In conversation

Franklin Furnace interviewed me about the show on Instagram. Watch the reel.

Franklin Furnace has been so crucial to helping bring this production to life. Their FUND for Performance Art grant was the main motivating factor for me deciding to go for it in putting on a self-produced show of this scale. So many great artists working at the edges of all forms of performance have been supported by this organization and I feel incredibly lucky to be one of them.
