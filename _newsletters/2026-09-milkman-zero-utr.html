MILKMAN ZERO is part of Under the Radar 2027

Hi!

Two weeks ago I told you MILKMAN ZERO premieres in December. Today I get to tell you it's coming back in January as part of the Under the Radar Festival.

Under the Radar is the festival I've looked forward to every January since I moved to New York. I was part of it in 2024 with Bag of Worms, and getting to participate once again means a lot. Presented by Mitu and Under the Radar, the run is six more performances, January 20 through 24, in the same theater at Mitu580 in Gowanus. Think of it as an encore run of the show :)

If you haven't read the last email: it's a solo performance built inside a text adventure. I sit at a keyboard, I type, and the story spells itself out on a video wall behind me. It's a darkly funny horror story about a milkman trying to deliver milk. It gets much weirder from there. Directed by Jerry Lieblich, with Kate McGee on lights and set and Ryan Gamblin on sound.

Also, in my previous newsletter I made the unfortunate mistake of leaving a crucial member of our team out of the credits: Jeremy Kadetsky on stage management. They won't be with us for the UTR run, since they have a fantastic opportunity lined up for those dates and we're excited for them, but they're on the December world premiere team.

On that note, December is still the world premiere and still on sale. So now there are two ways in. December tickets are here, and January tickets are through Under the Radar. Either way, the code CHUG gets you $25 tickets to any performance. And heads up: the $20 early bird tickets for the first week of the December run are nearly gone.

Same ask as last time: word of mouth is how people find this show. If you know someone who'd like this, forward them this email, send them to milkmanzero.com, or share it on Instagram.

See you in December. Or January.

Matt

MILKMAN ZERO

by Matt Romein  ·  directed by Jerry Lieblich

One performer. One keyboard. One job. Deliver the milk…

World Premiere · December 3–13, 2026
Under the Radar Festival · January 20–24, 2027

Presented in partnership with Under the Radar · utrfest.org

You are a milkman. You have a job to do. Deliver the milk. But the townspeople are insatiably milk-hungry, the mayor's eyes are brimming with milk-lust, and lurking in the milk-dark shadows, the Milk Monster stalks your every move. Still, you want to do a good job. Maybe even a great job…

MILKMAN ZERO is a darkly funny work of psychological horror built inside a text adventure, the oldest kind of computer game. No graphics, no images, just a world conjured with text and a blinking cursor waiting for the next command. Matt Romein plays the game live from a keyboard as the story spells itself out letter by letter on an LED wall behind him, every character landing with the clack of a typewriter. Like a ghost story around a campfire, the horror takes shape in the audience's collective imagination, one milk delivery at a time.

Work-in-progress showing at CATCH, The Invisible Dog Art Center, 2025. Photo: Maria Baranova.

Part solo performance, part live playthrough: over the course of an evening, the world of MILKMAN ZERO continues to exist only as long as the player keeps typing.
